Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Heat Sculpt Teacher Training in Portugal.

What is Heat Sculpt Teacher Training?

A 3-day in-person certification for yoga and Pilates instructors who want to teach high-intensity, music-driven sculpt classes. Next dates are January 29-31, 2027 in Lagos. The September 2026 training is SOLD OUT. Led by Alizee at Heat Lagos.

How much does it cost?

Heat Sculpt Teacher Training is €490 early bird or €550 after 15 July 2026. Tuition only. Travel, accommodation, and meals are not included.

Who is the training for?

Yoga, Pilates, and fitness instructors who want a hireable, low-impact strength format. You do not need to be a professional dancer. You do need a body-aware teaching practice and a willingness to coach with energy.

Do I need to be flexible or advanced?

No. SCULPT is about precision, endurance, and coaching — not advanced poses. You need a consistent movement practice and a genuine desire to teach the method well.

What is the schedule?

Three full days at Heat Lagos, January 29-31, 2027. Day 1 covers method foundations. Day 2 covers programming and teaching skills. Day 3 covers musicality, client experience, and practice teaching. See the full curriculum.

Can I teach sculpt after this training?

Yes. You leave with a Heat Sculpt Teacher Training certificate and the tools to programme, cue, and hold a class. Many graduates add sculpt to an existing studio or freelance schedule immediately.

Where is the training held?

All trainings are held at Heat Lagos in Lagos, Algarve, Portugal. Edificio da Fabrica da Ribeira, Av. dos Descobrimentos, directly across from Praia de Batata. Lagos is about 75 minutes from Faro Airport.

Is accommodation included?

No. Accommodation, travel, and meals are not included. This is intentional — you choose your own lodging so you can rest and enjoy Lagos your way. Many students stay in nearby Airbnbs, surf houses, or hotels. We can recommend options at various budgets.
