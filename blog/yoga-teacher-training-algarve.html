Teacher Training in the Algarve, Portugal

The Algarve is better known for golf and beach holidays than for teacher training. That is changing. Here is what Heat Sculpt Teacher Training in Lagos actually looks like, and why the location matters.

Where in the Algarve

Heat Sculpt Teacher Training takes place at Heat Lagos, a boutique infrared studio on Avenida dos Descobrimentos in Lagos. The studio is on the western end of the Algarve, five minutes on foot from the town centre, directly across from Praia de Batata.

Lagos is a different environment from the more tourist-heavy resorts further east. It is a working town with a small city feel: independent restaurants, a functioning market, a covered old town, and beaches within ten minutes of the centre. During an intensive training, the ability to step outside between sessions and be somewhere real rather than a resort strip matters more than most people anticipate.

The training itself

Heat Sculpt is a 3-day in-person certification for yoga and Pilates instructors. Next dates: January 29-31, 2027. The September 2026 training is SOLD OUT. Led by Alizee, it covers method foundations, programming, cueing, musicality, and practice teaching.

The training is held in a fully equipped infrared-heated studio. Infrared heat supports deeper work: muscles stay warm through long session days, which reduces injury risk and supports the physical demand of a compact certification.

Why the Algarve

Environment changes the quality of learning. When you remove yourself from your routine, you create space for something different to happen. Intensive training works because of this immersion, not despite it.

The Algarve in late January is mild by northern European standards. The Atlantic is right there. Between sessions you can walk, swim if you are brave, or sit in a cafe and process what you have absorbed. Recovery between sessions is part of how deep learning works.

Practically: Lagos is easier to reach than anything outside Europe. Direct flights from the UK, Germany, Scandinavia, and the Netherlands connect to Faro, about 75 kilometres away.

Getting to Lagos

The nearest airport is Faro (FAO). From Faro to Lagos: the train is the most scenic option (approximately 2 hours, change at Tunes). A taxi or transfer takes around 75 minutes.

Once in Lagos, the studio is within walking distance of most accommodation in the centre. Having a car is not necessary for a 3-day training.

Where to stay

We do not include accommodation because students have different needs and budgets. Lagos has Airbnbs, surf houses, guesthouses, and hotels within walking distance of the studio. See our Lagos accommodation guide.
