Your Teachers

Alizee leads Heat Sculpt. Stine and Sebastian run Heat Lagos — the studio where you train.

Alizee

Lead SCULPT Trainer · ALIZEE STUDIO

Coming from a strong dance and movement background, Alizee has spent years developing an approach that combines precision, strength, control, and fluidity. After 15 years of ballet, she continued in contemporary dance while deepening mobility, conditioning, and teaching.

Her classes are known for intensity, precision, and intelligent sequencing. Heat Sculpt uses resistance bands, ankle weights, and light weights for low-impact, high-intensity results.

She leads the 3-day Heat Sculpt Teacher Training at Heat Lagos — built for yoga, Pilates, and fitness instructors who want to offer a powerful new format.

SCULPT Method Programming Musicality Coaching

Stine

Heat Lagos · 15+ Years Teaching

Stine is the heartbeat of Heat Lagos. She has spent over 15 years refining how to hold space, read a room, and build classes that serve the people in front of you.

Her teaching is grounded in anatomy, breath, and the practical reality of running a studio day after day. She hosts Heat Sculpt Teacher Training at the Lagos studio and supports students on logistics, recovery, and life in the Algarve.

Sebastian

Heat Lagos · World BJJ Champion · Movement

Sebastian came to movement through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — a world where mobility, recovery, and breath control matter. He brings an athlete's perspective to Heat Lagos and to how bodies actually work under load.

He also runs Yoga for BJJ, bringing yoga to martial artists worldwide.
