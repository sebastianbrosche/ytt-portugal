SCULPT Curriculum

Three days. Practical skills you can teach on Monday. Not theory you forget on the flight home.

Programme Structure

Heat Sculpt Teacher Training is a compact, in-person certification at Heat Lagos in Lagos, Portugal.

  • ▸ 3 live days — January 29-31, 2027 (September 2026 is SOLD OUT)
  • ▸ Led by Alizee — method, programming, coaching, and musicality
  • ▸ For yoga and Pilates teachers — add a hireable, music-driven format

Day 1: Foundations & Method

Dive into the SCULPT method through a signature showcase class, movement foundations, core principles, technique, alignment, and sequencing basics.

  • Signature SCULPT class as student and observer
  • Movement language: precision, control, and flow
  • Props: bands, ankle weights, and light dumbbells
  • Alignment and joint-friendly loading
  • How SCULPT differs from yoga, Pilates, and HIIT

Day 2: Programming & Teaching Skills

Build classes that make sense — not a random playlist of exercises. Learn to coach with clarity, energy, and confidence.

  • Sequencing and layering methodology
  • Class architecture: warm-up, peak work, cool-down
  • Cueing: action, alignment, and rhythm
  • Modifications for different bodies and levels
  • Practice teaching in small groups

Day 3: Practice & Communication

Musicality, client experience, and the presence that makes a class feel like a brand — not a workout dump.

  • Music as structure, not background
  • Holding space and reading a room
  • Full teach-backs with feedback
  • How to launch SCULPT in your studio or schedule
